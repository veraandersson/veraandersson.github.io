I am a PhD Candidate in **Mathematical Statistics at the University of Maryland, College Park**. My research focuses on the intersection of graph theory and statistical inference, specifically investigating how structural properties of networks can be recovered under noise or shuffling. My current work, advised by **[Dr. Vince Lyzinski](https://www.math.umd.edu/~vlyzinsk/)**, involves investigating how node shuffling impacts graph classification. 

Before moving to Maryland, I completed my B.Sc. and M.Sc. in **Mathematical Statistics at Stockholm University, Sweden**, where my research ranged from optimizing COVID-19 vaccination strategies (advised by **[Dr. Pieter Trapman](https://www.rug.nl/staff/j.p.trapman/?lang=en)**) to developing methods to simplify complex psychiatric networks (advised by **[Dr. Tom Britton](https://staff.math.su.se/tom.britton/)** and **[Dr. Lars Klintwall](https://ki.se/personer/lars-klintwall)**).
