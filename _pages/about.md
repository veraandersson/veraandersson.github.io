I am a PhD Candidate in **Mathematical Statistics at the University of Maryland, College Park**. My research focuses on the intersection of graph theory and statistical inference, specifically investigating how structural properties of networks can be recovered under noise or shuffling. My current work involves investigating how node shuffling impacts graph classification.

Before moving to Maryland, I completed my B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Mathematical Statistics at **Stockholm University, Sweden**, where my research ranged from optimizing COVID-19 vaccination strategies to developing methods to simplify complex psychiatric networks.
